A direct definition

An Intelligent PO is the living workspace for a retailer-issued purchase order.

It connects the systems, teams, documents, shipments, compliance checks, deductions, and decisions that move a CPG order from receipt through closeout. It complements ERP, WMS, EDI, email, and retailer portals rather than replacing them.
