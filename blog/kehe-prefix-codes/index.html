What the confidence levels mean

Verified

Validated at 100% accuracy across held-out test data. 11 codes are additionally confirmed against expert domain rulings from a 20-year KeHE operations professional.

High confidence

Anchored in KeHE P&P, Fee Schedule, IBR Guide, or other canonical source documents. Pending production-sample validation where noted.

Documented

Named in the KeHE Glossary (doc 039) or referenced in production data. Operational details may be incomplete.

Research

Identified in the prefix index but not yet fully documented. Often alias pairs, dormant codes, or retailer-specific variants with limited observation data.
