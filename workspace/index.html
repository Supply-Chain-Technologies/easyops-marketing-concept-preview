The category definition

The purchase order becomes the workspace.

An Intelligent PO™ is a living workspace for every retailer-issued purchase order. It connects the people, systems, requirements, evidence, and actions that move a CPG order from receipt to shelf, payment, and recovery.

Frequently asked

Understand the boundary before evaluating the product.

Is an Intelligent PO procurement software?+

No. Procurement software helps a buyer create and approve purchases. EasyOps is designed around the retailer-issued PO received by a CPG brand and the operational work that follows.

Does EasyOps replace EDI, ERP, WMS, or retailer portals?+

The concept is a coordination and intelligence layer. It connects context from existing systems so teams can see the complete operational record and act from one place. Final integration scope should be confirmed during implementation.

Who works from the PO?+

Operations, finance, sales, leadership, brokers, warehouses, 3PLs, and other collaborators may each contribute context or act on the same order.

Where do deductions fit?+

Deductions are part of the PO lifecycle, not a disconnected after-the-fact workflow. Evidence and decisions captured before fulfillment can support prevention and recovery later.
