Capabilities

What EasyOps does for operations.

📦

One workspace per PO

Every purchase order becomes a living workspace that collects the order details, shipment tracking, communications, compliance checks, deductions, and decisions in one place.

🔗

Connect, don't replace

EasyOps integrates with EDI providers, ERPs, WMS/3PLs, email, Slack, and distributor portals. Your team keeps using the tools they know. The workspace makes the connections between them visible.

👥

Shared context across roles

Operations, sales, finance, brokers, and 3PLs see the same PO context. No forwarding, no "can you check on that?", no information locked in one person's inbox.

Real scenarios

Before and after.

The context switch that cost $1,240

Before

A shortage deduction posts. The evidence is split across the EDI provider (ASN), the 3PL (BOL), email (dock delay thread), and the ERP (invoice). By the time someone connects all four, the UDR response window has closed.

With EasyOps

All five sources are connected to the PO the moment each event happens. When the deduction posts, the evidence packet is already assembled. The response files within the window.

The buyer email that no one on the ops team saw

Before

A buyer emails the sales team about a routing exception. Sales forwards it to ops. Ops doesn't see it until after the shipment has already been picked wrong.

With EasyOps

The email is automatically filed to the PO. The routing exception surfaces on the ops team's action list before the pick order is released.

Frequently asked

Questions about operations.

Does EasyOps replace my ERP or WMS?+

No. EasyOps connects to your ERP, WMS, EDI provider, email, and distributor portals via API integrations and webhooks. Each system keeps doing its job. The Intelligent PO adds the connective layer that makes the operational story visible across all of them.

How many team members can use it?+

Up to 19 seats are included at no additional cost. Anyone with an operational lens — ops, sales, finance, broker, 3PL — can see the same PO context.

How long does setup take?+

Days, not weeks. EasyOps connects to your existing systems via API integrations and webhooks. There is no rip-and-replace project. Onboarding focuses on connecting your specific workflow and building the custom compliance rules for your distributor relationships.

What does the AI agent actually do?+

It evaluates the total context of each PO — order details, shipment status, communications, compliance requirements, and deduction history — and surfaces what needs attention, what the risk is, and what the next action should be. It does not make decisions for your team. It gives your team the context to make better decisions faster.
