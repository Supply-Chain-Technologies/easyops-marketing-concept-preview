Frequently asked

Questions about deductions.

What percentage of KeHE deductions are actually disputable?+

In our experience, the majority of deduction dollars are contractual passthrough. In one audit, 88% ($140,914 of $151,671) was contractual. The remaining 12% contained $5,940 in estimated recoverable value. The audit's job is to separate the two so effort goes where it matters.

How long do I have to dispute a KeHE deduction?+

KeHE requires disputes filed through K-Solve within 180 days of the deduction date. K-Solve quick search covers roughly the last 90 days. Current KeHE portal instructions govern the actual deadlines.

Does EasyOps file disputes automatically?+

For KeHE and UNFI portals, yes — automated dispute submission is available. For other portals, the system prepares the dispute text and evidence packet for copy-paste submission. The goal is either path results in a filed dispute, not an unfiled one.

How is this different from Glimpse or OverDeduct?+

Most deduction tools start at the deduction and work backward. EasyOps starts at the purchase order and works forward — connecting the systems, evidence, and communications that prevent deductions from happening, then recovering what still slips through. Prevention is where the economics shift.
