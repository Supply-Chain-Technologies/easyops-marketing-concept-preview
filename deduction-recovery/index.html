The audit

From K-Solve spreadsheet to money back

K-Solve tells you what was taken. It doesn't tell you why, whether it was valid, or how to get it back. The audit does, automatically.

  • Explains every deduction code: ~100 invoice prefixes, straight from the KeHE glossary
  • Finds the recoverable ones and estimates the dollars
  • Matches each deduction to the PO and the rule version it cites
  • Prioritizes what to dispute first, with the evidence to attach
