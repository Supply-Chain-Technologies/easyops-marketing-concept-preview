Interactive architecture guide

Your systems move data. Who keeps the operational story?

An Intelligent PO does not ask every system to become something it is not. It connects their context around the order your team must execute.

Retailer & distributor

01

Issues the PO, requirements, portals, appointments, remittance, and deductions.

EDI provider

02

Transports structured transactions such as the 850, 855, 856, 810, and 812.

ERP / accounting

03

Holds financial records, items, customers, invoices, and accounting truth.

WMS / 3PL

04

Executes inventory, picking, packing, shipping, and warehouse evidence.

Email & team tools

05

Carry exceptions, decisions, attachments, follow-ups, and human context.

Intelligent PO

06

Connects the operational story, requirements, evidence, attention, and next actions around each retailer-issued PO.

A practical boundary test

If the question is…

Did the 856 transmit?

Start with EDI.

If the question is…

What inventory shipped?

Start with WMS or 3PL.

If the question is…

What happened, who owns it, and what proves it?

Start with the PO workspace.

What to ask in an evaluation

  1. 01Does the product organize work around retailer-issued POs?
  2. 02Can it connect evidence without forcing a rip-and-replace project?
  3. 03Does it show what needs attention and why?
  4. 04Can it preserve the source behind a compliance or deduction decision?
  5. 05Does it support prevention as well as recovery?
  6. 06Can finance, operations, sales, and external partners share context safely?
