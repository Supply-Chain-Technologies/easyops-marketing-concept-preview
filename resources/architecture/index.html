The stack

Six systems, six different jobs, one shared PO.

Retailer & distributor

01

Issues the PO, requirements, portals, appointments, remittance, and deductions. KeHE, UNFI, Target, Walmart, and other retailers each have their own portal and compliance rules.

EDI provider

02

Transports structured transactions: 850 (PO), 855 (acknowledgement), 856 (ASN), 810 (invoice), 812 (credit memo). SPS Commerce and other providers carry the data but do not interpret it operationally.

ERP / accounting

03

Holds financial records, items, customers, invoices, and accounting truth. QuickBooks, NetSuite, and similar systems are the ledger, but they do not track the operational journey of a PO.

WMS / 3PL

04

Executes inventory, picking, packing, shipping, and warehouse evidence. The warehouse knows what shipped. It does not know why a deduction happened downstream.

Email & team tools

05

Carry exceptions, decisions, attachments, follow-ups, and human context. Critical operational information lives in Slack threads and email chains that no other system can see.

Intelligent PO

06

Connects the operational story, requirements, evidence, attention, and next actions around each retailer-issued PO. It does not replace any system above. It makes the connections between them visible and actionable.

A practical boundary test

Each system owns a specific question. The Intelligent PO exists because the most important operational questions span all of them.

If the question is…

Did the 856 transmit?

Start with EDI.

The EDI provider confirms whether the advance ship notice was sent and acknowledged.

If the question is…

What inventory shipped?

Start with WMS or 3PL.

The warehouse system knows the pick, pack, ship, and BOL details.

If the question is…

What is the financial record?

Start with the ERP.

The accounting system holds invoices, credit memos, and ledger entries.

If the question is…

What happened, who owns it, and what proves it?

Start with the PO workspace.

The operational story spans multiple systems. The Intelligent PO connects that context.

The operating model

Work it. Prevent it. Recover it.

The platform where your systems, teams, and operations work as one. Surface current status and risk. Coordinate the next action. Preserve the complete wholesale source of truth.

Keep your team in context

Orders, emails, documents, shipments, deductions, and decisions in one place.

Catch issues early

Flags price, timing, routing, and compliance mismatches before fulfillment.

Build evidence automatically

Audit record and source of truth without manual uploads or entry.

Automate deduction recovery

Drafts disputes, submits where possible, tracks every dispute through resolution.

Evaluation checklist

What to ask in an evaluation

Use these questions with any vendor, including EasyOps. The answers will reveal whether a solution connects your existing stack or creates another silo.

  1. 01Does the product organize work around retailer-issued POs?
  2. 02Can it connect evidence without forcing a rip-and-replace project?
  3. 03Does it show what needs attention and why, with full context?
  4. 04Can it preserve the source behind a compliance or deduction decision?
  5. 05Does it support prevention as well as recovery?
  6. 06Can finance, operations, sales, and external partners share context safely?
  7. 07Does it integrate with distributor portals, EDI, email, and team tools you already use?
  8. 08Does it track the complete lifecycle from PO receipt through payment and deduction resolution?
