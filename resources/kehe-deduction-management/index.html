Cornerstone guide · KeHE operations

A deduction is a line item. Managing it requires the whole operational story.

KeHE deduction management is the repeatable process of connecting a deduction to its purchase order, cause, evidence, decision, and outcome, then using what happened to reduce preventable recurrence.

The operating loop

From remittance to root cause in six controlled steps.

STEP 01

Find the deduction

Start with the remittance or K-Solve export. Preserve the original line, amount, date, code, and available reference fields.

STEP 02

Connect the PO

Match the line to the retailer-issued purchase order, shipment, invoice, and receiving event. Unmatched items stay in investigation.

STEP 03

Classify the cause

Separate contractual passthroughs from deductions that may be disputable. Do not treat every deduction as recoverable.

STEP 04

Assemble evidence

Collect the BOL, POD, packing list, ASN timing, appointment records, photos, emails, and applicable rule context.

STEP 05

Choose the action

Verify, dispute, request documentation, escalate, or accept. Record the reason and owner for the next step.

STEP 06

Close the loop

Track the outcome and feed recurring causes back into routing, documentation, and fulfillment work.

Evidence chain

Build the case around the PO, not inside another folder.

01Order

PO number, order date, ship window, requested delivery date, item and quantity detail

02Shipment

BOL, carrier events, appointment history, POD, pallet or case documentation

03Transaction

Invoice, remittance advice, K-Solve line, deduction code, allowance terms

04Communication

Buyer, broker, 3PL, warehouse, carrier, and KeHE correspondence

05Rule context

Current supplier policy, routing guide, fee schedule, or written agreement

Decision discipline

Three states. Three different next actions.

01

Contractual

The amount follows an agreed term or documented passthrough.

Verify the calculation, retain the record, and avoid spending dispute capacity without contrary evidence.

02

Disputable

The charge appears inconsistent with the PO, shipment record, agreement, or applicable rule.

Package the strongest evidence and file through the appropriate path within the current deadline.

03

Unknown

The line cannot yet be tied to enough context to make a defensible decision.

Request documentation, resolve the PO match, or escalate. Do not guess.

Frequently asked

Should every KeHE deduction be disputed?+

No. A sound process distinguishes contractual passthroughs, potentially disputable items, and cases that still lack enough information.

What should be preserved before a dispute?+

Preserve the original deduction line and connect it to the PO, invoice, remittance, shipment records, communications, and applicable rule context.

Where does K-Solve fit?+

K-Solve is an important deduction and dispute surface. It does not replace the operational evidence held across EDI, email, the 3PL, carrier records, and internal decisions.

How does prevention connect to recovery?+

The same evidence used to investigate a deduction can reveal repeatable breakdowns in timing, documentation, routing, or coordination. Those causes should feed upstream operating changes.

Evidence boundary: This guide is an operating framework, not a substitute for current KeHE documents or a determination that any specific deduction is recoverable. Always verify current terms, evidence, and deadlines.
