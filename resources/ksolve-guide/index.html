KeHE field guide

From a K-Solve line to an operational next step.

K-Solve shows that money was deducted. The investigation still has to connect the line to the order, governing requirement, operational event, and evidence.

Concept guidance only. KeHE source documents and current portal instructions govern.

1

Export

+

Start with the relevant K-Solve deduction data and preserve the original file.

2

Normalize

+

Connect each line to the PO, invoice, deduction date, amount, and reason code available.

3

Classify

+

Separate likely contractual pass-throughs from issues that may warrant investigation or dispute.

4

Trace

+

Identify the governing requirement, transaction, shipment event, or document behind the deduction.

5

Gather

+

Bring together BOLs, packing lists, photos, EDI records, appointment evidence, and messages.

6

Prioritize

+

Order the work by value, evidence readiness, deadlines, and likely operational impact.

7

Act

+

File or route the next step using the required channel, then track status through resolution.

8

Learn

+

Carry the root cause back into the PO workflow to reduce repeat issues.
