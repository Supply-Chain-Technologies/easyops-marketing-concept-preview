The strongest answer may be a combination.

Software can provide shared context and repeatable workflow. People still make decisions, manage relationships, and handle exceptions. A service may add specialized execution for a defined scope. The evaluation should begin with which jobs are recurring, which require judgment, and which are currently being abandoned.
