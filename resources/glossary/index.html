Reference layer

The language of CPG wholesale operations.

Stable, plain-language definitions help operators, buyers, and machines understand the same workflow.

Intelligent PO™
A living workspace organized around a retailer-issued purchase order and the people, systems, requirements, evidence, and actions needed to execute it.
Retailer-issued PO
A purchase order created by a retailer or distributor and received by the supplying CPG brand.
PO-to-shelf
The operational journey from receiving a retailer-issued PO through acknowledgement, fulfillment, shipping, receiving, payment, deductions, and closeout.
Deduction
An amount withheld or subtracted from payment, which may reflect a contractual allowance, fee, compliance issue, shortage, damage, pricing issue, or other reason.
Deduction evidence
Documents and system records used to explain, validate, or challenge a deduction, such as BOLs, packing lists, photos, EDI records, appointments, and messages.
K-Solve
KeHE’s supplier-facing environment used for financial and deduction-related workflows. Current KeHE instructions govern its use.
UDR
A workflow or document associated with delivery or receiving discrepancies. Current distributor documentation governs deadlines and procedures.
EDI
Electronic Data Interchange, the structured exchange of business documents such as purchase orders, acknowledgements, shipment notices, invoices, and credit information.
ERP
Enterprise Resource Planning or accounting software used to manage financial, item, customer, invoice, and other business records.
WMS
Warehouse Management System software used to manage inventory and warehouse execution.
3PL
A third-party logistics provider that performs warehousing, fulfillment, transportation, or related operations for a brand.
Compliance mismatch
A difference between an order or shipment and the applicable retailer or distributor requirement.

Definitions describe the EasyOps concept and general operations context. Retailer and distributor source documents govern their specific programs, deadlines, and requirements.
